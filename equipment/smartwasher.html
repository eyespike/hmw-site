Description

CRC SmartWasher mobile parts washing system. Unlike a traditional solvent washer, the SmartWasher uses OzzyJuice degreasing solution and the OzzyMat — microbes that continuously break down grease and oil into water and carbon dioxide. The result is high-performance cleaning that is non-hazardous, non-flammable, non-corrosive, and non-polluting, with no hazardous solvent waste to dispose of.

Wheeled cart design rolls anywhere in the shop. Includes the heated recirculating basin, flexible flow brush, and on-board fluid reservoir. Currently set up and ready to use.
